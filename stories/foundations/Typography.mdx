# Typography

IBM Plex Sans (variable, 100 to 700) and IBM Plex Mono 400, self-hosted through Fontsource, with metric-matched fallbacks.

Type scale close to 1.25: 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 20, 24, 30, 38, 48, 80 px (in rem in CSS).
Line height: 1.1 headings, 1.5 ledes and code, 1.65 text. H1, H2 and the lede are fluid with `clamp()`.
